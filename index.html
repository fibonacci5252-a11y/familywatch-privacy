FamilyWatch

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In plain words FamilyWatch does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All filtering happens locally on your device. Nothing you do online is ever sent to us.

1. What FamilyWatch does

FamilyWatch is a Chrome browser extension that blocks access to adult and inappropriate websites. It is designed for families with children and educational institutions. The extension works entirely on your device — no account is required, and no browsing data is transmitted externally.

2. Data we do not collect

FamilyWatch does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, age, address)
  • Browsing history or visited URLs
  • Authentication credentials or passwords
  • Location data or IP address
  • Device information or usage analytics
  • Web page content

3. Data stored locally on your device

The following information is stored locally in your browser using the Chrome storage API. It never leaves your device:

  • The domain blocklist (a list of websites to block)
  • The parental password, stored as a SHA-256 hash — the original password is never stored
  • Your extension preferences and settings

4. Permissions used and why

Permission Purpose Data sent externally?
declarativeNetRequest Blocks requests to domains on the blocklist, entirely within the browser No
storage Saves the blocklist, password hash, and settings locally No
notifications Alerts parents locally when a blocked site is accessed No
alarms Schedules the automatic blocklist refresh every 24 hours No

5. Blocklist updates

Once every 24 hours, the extension downloads an updated list of domains from a public GitHub repository. This request contains no user data — only a version number is checked to avoid unnecessary downloads. The downloaded file contains only a list of domain names, not executable code.

6. Children's privacy

FamilyWatch is designed to protect children, not to collect data about them. The extension does not collect any information from or about children. No data related to minors is processed, stored, or transmitted.

7. Third-party services

FamilyWatch does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external connection is the blocklist update described in section 5, hosted on GitHub.

Premium features (email alerts) use Firebase and SendGrid solely to deliver notifications you have configured. These services receive only the email address you provide and the notification content — no browsing data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the extension after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the FamilyWatch GitHub repository.
